The weaver's foot works the treadle, and the shuttle flies through the warp. The beater swings home with a soft knock.

The treadle slows. The shuttle crosses again, unhurried, and the beater falls gently. Light lies across the half-finished cloth.

Once more the shuttle crosses, slower still. The weaver's hands rest on the beam.

Between the threads, the shuttle lies still. The cloth waits in the light.
