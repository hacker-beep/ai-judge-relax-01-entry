The baker opens the oven door and the heat rolls out. Inside, the loaf has split along the top, golden at the edges.

The fire has burned to embers. One small flame still moves along a log, slower now, and the glow deepens to red.

The flame shrinks to a blue edge and goes still. The embers breathe their last light.

On the stone, the loaf lies still, its crust set and gleaming. The oven holds its warmth.
