At the back of the bakery, a loaf cools on a wooden rack. Its crust gives a small crackle. Warm air carries the smell of toasted flour through the room.

The baker brushes the table with the side of her hand. Flour gathers in a pale crescent beside the loaf. She hangs her apron on its peg; the strings sway, then lie against the door.

Another faint tick from the crust. A longer quiet. In the split along the top, the bread is soft and pale.

One grain of flour slips from the rack. It lands without a sound.
