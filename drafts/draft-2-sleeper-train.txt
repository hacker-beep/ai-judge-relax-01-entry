The sleeping carriage rounds a long bend. Amber light rests on the folded blanket. In its little net beside the berth, an orange rocks once, then settles against the wool.

Beyond the window, a farmhouse light drifts between two dark hills. The wheels pass over a join in the rails. A low sound, then the long even hum again.

The curtain stirs against the glass. Its hem lifts clear of the sill, pauses, and falls into the same soft fold.

The orange stays in its nest. The hills move on.
