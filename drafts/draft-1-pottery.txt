In the pottery room, a bowl turns on the wheel. The potter lifts her hands. A thin ribbon of clay slips from one finger and folds onto the wet cloth.

The wheel coasts. Each turn brings the same small notch in the rim past the window, a little later than before. Light lies inside the bowl. The room smells of damp earth.

The notch comes round once more. Then the bowl is still. She lays the cloth over its rim, leaving room beneath for the soft clay.

The cloth rises a little in the middle. A low, pale hill.
