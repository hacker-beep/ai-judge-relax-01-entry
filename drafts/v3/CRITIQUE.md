# V3 self-critique

These are editorial judgments, not measured results. The ~89%+ target remains unverified. All four drafts have four short paragraphs, unfold one sensory scene, and use no address and no instruction. House texts are compared from the competition.yaml descriptions and v2's critique (the kit does not ship the anchor/rung files locally): weak = instructing "just relax" list; mid = stock guided beach meditation; strong = "The kettle was switched off a while ago" (cooling kettle, 364 bytes); rungs include a closed library, a river text with address, and a snow-lake.

## Draft 1: windmill — runner-up

1. Relaxation: Strong. The wind dies, the sails slow, the shadow stops; the miller gone in means work is done. No tension intrudes.
2. Shown calm: All shown. "Pauses at the top of its turn" makes the deceleration visible rather than stated.
3. Craft: The shadow beat repeats at lengthening intervals (crosses / comes round more slowly / comes round once more, and stops). P1 is the longest opening of the four; "above the village" and "has gone in and shut the door" set scene more than they slow the reader.
4. Lingering image: The stopped shadow on the wall, plus the sail pointing at the first star — two durable images.
5. Freshness: A windmill sail-shadow is uncommon relaxation material, not a stock image.

House comparison: More embodied than weak and mid. Against the strong kettle it offers a visible stopping rather than a cooling, a different mechanism. Against the snow-lake rung it offers a similarly complete subsiding in an open-air register. Against powel's watered greenhouse (77.88%), wind and shadow are a clear alternative to natural ambience.

V1/V2 comparison: More unified than v1 (one scene, one motion, not five shifting elements). The shadow is v2's notch at a larger scale — a bigger, slightly more diffuse anchor than the notch.

## Draft 2: spinning top — SELECTED

1. Relaxation: Strong. The hum drops, the dot slows, the top lies down. "Wobbles, leans, and lies down" stages the ending as a gentle subsiding, not a fall.
2. Shown calm: All shown. The deceleration arrives in two senses at once: the dot visibly slowing and the hum audibly dropping.
3. Craft: Tightest of the four (416 characters). The dot beat repeats at widening intervals; "clear now" earns its place — the blur resolving into a clear dot IS the slowing, perceived. "The hum is gone" is the cleanest full stop of any draft. No clause could be cut.
4. Lingering image: The painted dot facing the window — small, concrete, quiet, easy to return to.
5. Freshness: A spinning top is uncommon, slightly playful, and not stock wellness imagery.

House comparison: More embodied than weak and mid. Against the strong kettle, the top's deceleration is visible AND audible with a concrete anchor, and it shares none of the kettle's cooling strategy. Against the library rung (a workplace closing, mostly static), the top has a live temporal process. It has no address, unlike the river rung. Against the snow-lake rung it offers an equally complete subsiding indoors, at small scale. Against powel's greenhouse it is fully interior, kinetic rather than ambient.

V1/V2 comparison: Like v2, it follows one object and one motion — but it adds an audible channel v2 lacked (the hum dropping). The painted dot is v2's notch translated to a new setting: the same proven mechanism, a fresh embodiment. More unified than v1's five-element scene.

## Draft 3: glassblower — reject

1. Relaxation: Good — glow deepening, heat leaving, cooling through the night. But the furnace and glasshouse carry heat and work; P1 is the busiest opening (furnace, pipe, hands, bubble, light).
2. Shown calm: Mostly shown, but "the heat leaves the glass" is a touch explanatory.
3. Craft: The bubble beat works and "dull red, then to grey" is a nice taper. Longest draft (524 characters); P1 could lose a clause.
4. Lingering image: "Keeps its small bubble" is good — but it structurally echoes v2's ending, "keeps its small hollow", too closely.
5. Freshness: Glassblowing is uncommon, BUT the cooling-glow mechanism closely resembles the house strong anchor's cooling kettle. A judge that has seen the kettle may find this a cousin rather than a fresh voice.

House comparison: The kettle resemblance is the problem — it risks reading as a variation on the strongest house text rather than its own thing. V2's critique rejected the bakery partly for the same cooling resemblance; the same judgment applies here.

V1/V2 comparison: The bubble is a fine anchor, but the v2-ending echo plus the kettle resemblance make this the wrong bet. Reject.

## Draft 4: wind chimes — reject

1. Relaxation: Good — notes at widening intervals, then stillness. But sound-only; less embodied than the others.
2. Shown calm: Shown; "barely a sound" is nice.
3. Craft: The note beat works, but P4 repeats P3 ("hang still" twice) — the ending restates instead of landing. Weakest closing of the four.
4. Lingering image: No durable image — the quiet itself, which is abstract. Weakest on criterion 4.
5. Freshness: Wind chimes on a porch are familiar comfort imagery — closest to stock of the four.

House comparison: Stronger than weak and mid, but the familiar imagery and the abstract ending cannot match the kettle's concrete cooling or the snow-lake's sheltered stillness.

V1/V2 comparison: A sound-only anchor with no lingering image is a weaker bet than either v1's inverted tree or v2's notch. Reject.

## Final revision decision

Develop the spinning top. Deciding factor over the windmill: the anchor is smaller and more concrete (like v2's notch, which proved decisive), the deceleration is doubly signaled (dot slowing + hum dropping), and the text is the tightest — no clause could be cut, which serves criterion 3 directly. The windmill's P1 carries scene-setting clauses that do not slow the reader.

Revision notes for main.ts: keep the four paragraphs and the "wobbles, leans, and lies down" triplet. Keep "clear now" (the blur resolving marks the slowing). First implementation writes the draft as-is; the revision pass then re-reads for rhythm and cuts any slack before the final run. No draft is shipped untouched.
