In the glasshouse, the blower lifts the pipe from the furnace and the gather glows orange at its end. He turns the pipe slowly in his hands, and a small bubble in the glass catches the light.

The pipe turns, and the bubble comes round again, more slowly now. The glow deepens from orange to red, and the heat leaves the glass.

The bubble comes round once more, and the pipe rests on the bench. The glass darkens to a dull red, then to grey.

In the annealer, the piece cools through the night, and keeps its small bubble.
