On the hill above the village, the windmill's sails turn in the last of the evening wind. The miller has gone in and shut the door. One sail's shadow crosses the whitewashed wall, and crosses it again.

The wind drops. The shadow comes round more slowly now, and pauses at the top of its turn before it moves on.

The shadow comes round once more, and stops halfway across the wall. The sails rest with one arm pointing at the first star.

On the wall, the shadow stays where it stopped.
